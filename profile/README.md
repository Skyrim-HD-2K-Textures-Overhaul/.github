# Skyrim HD - 2K Textures: High-Resolution Landscape, Architecture, and Environment Texture Overhaul

Skyrim HD - 2K Textures is a texture overhaul mod for The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim, created by nebula to enhance the visual quality of the game world. It replaces over 600 textures and normal maps across landscapes, architecture, dungeons, water, skies, and clothing. With FULL and LITE editions and customizable texture packages, players can improve Skyrim's visuals while choosing resolutions that suit their hardware.

## Environmental Textures and Visual Improvements

The replacement collection extends beyond conventional landscape and building textures. It also includes several environmental effects and smaller world assets that contribute to the overall appearance of Skyrim.

Notable texture categories include:

- **Landscapes:** Roads, mountains, cliffs, glaciers, beaches, and other terrain surfaces.
- **Architecture:** City buildings, walls, farmhouse materials, floors, and structural details.
- **Water:** Water surfaces, rain ripples, water fog, streams, and foam.
- **Sky:** Stars, galaxy textures, and sunglare.
- **Dungeon environments:** Dungeon ground surfaces and Dwemer architecture.
- **Clutter and furniture:** Barrels, carts, firewood, workbenches, furniture, and blacksmith objects.
- **Clothing:** Blacksmith and barkeeper clothing textures.
- **Reflections and LOD:** Improved reflective surfaces and distant landscape textures.
